Course descriptions

Logic Detective

Lessons

  • The Mystery of Patterns
  • Who Stole the Cookies?
  • Find the Odd One Out
  • The Secret Sequence
  • The Final Detective Challenge

Task types: logic puzzles, pattern matching, “find the odd one”, quizzes, detective challenges.

Math Adventures

Lessons:

  • Numbers Around Us
  • The Magic of Addition
  • Number Challenges
  • Geometry Around Us
  • The Great Math Quest

Task types: mathematical puzzles, calculations, drag-and-drop, multiple-choice questions, timed challenges.

Little Scientist

Lessons:

  • Why Is the Rainbow Colorful?
  • The Magic of Magnets
  • Light and Shadows
  • What Makes Things Float?
  • What Happens If...?

Task types: interactive experiments, “What will happen?” questions, true or false, predictions, quizzes.

World Explorer

Lessons:

  • Our Amazing Planet
  • Continents and Oceans
  • A Trip Around the World
  • Amazing Places
  • Meet Different Cultures

Task types: interactive maps, quizzes, matching tasks, “guess the place”, geography quests.

Animal Planet

Lessons:

  • Who Lives Where?
  • Animal Superpowers
  • Wild or Domestic?
  • The Animal Food Chain
  • Guess the Animal

Task types: picture quizzes, matching animals to habitats, riddles, “guess who”, sorting tasks.

Eco Heroes

Lessons:

  • Our Planet Needs Us
  • Save Water, Save the Planet
  • Where Does Waste Go?
  • Sort It Right!
  • Become an Eco Hero

Task types: sorting games, situational tasks, quizzes, drag-and-drop, eco challenges.

Code Quest

Lessons:

  • What Is an Algorithm?
  • Give Instructions to a Robot
  • Build Your First Algorithm
  • Find the Mistake
  • Mission: Program a Robot

Task types: arrange-the-steps tasks, algorithm puzzles, drag-and-drop, error detection, interactive challenges.

Smart Money

Lessons:

  • What Is Money?
  • Need or Want?
  • Build Your First Budget
  • How to Save for a Dream
  • Smart Shopper Challenge

Task types: situational games, budgeting tasks, multiple-choice questions, decision-making challenges, quizzes.

Story Quest

Lessons:

  • Meet the Characters
  • Put the Story in Order
  • Find the Hidden Meaning
  • Create a New Ending
  • Become a Storyteller

Task types: text-based quizzes, story sequencing, matching, creative writing tasks, comprehension questions.

Creative Lab

Lessons:

  • The World of Colors
  • Shapes and Imagination
  • Create Your Own Character
  • Design Your Fantasy World
  • The Big Creative Challenge

Task types: creative challenges, image-based tasks, color matching, drawing prompts, “create your own” assignments.

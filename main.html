"EDU SPARK" - Children's Learning Portal

Portal goal

Creating an accessible and interesting digital environment for children's education, in which acquiring new knowledge is combined with play, research and interactive interaction

Tasks to achieve the goal:

  • to interest children in learning through game and interactive formats;
  • to create a selection of short and understandable educational courses for children;
  • to develop logical, critical and creative thinking;
  • to help children apply the knowledge they have gained in practice;
  • to provide the opportunity to independently complete lessons and test their own knowledge;
  • to motivate children to further study using a system of points, achievements and awards.
